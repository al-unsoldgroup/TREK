Trip Advice Privacy Policy

Effective 14 September 2026

Unsold Group operates Trip Advice in the United States. This policy explains how Trip Advice handles information when a trip owner shares an itinerary and guests contribute advice.

Information we handle

Trip Advice processes the itinerary details selected by the trip owner. Guest information can include votes, comments, place suggestions, an optional display name, and a session identifier stored in the browser. Service logs may include an IP address, browser details, timestamps, and security events.

How we use information

We use this information to display the shared trip, deliver contributions to the owner, prevent abuse, protect the service, diagnose failures, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Google Places

When you use place search or request a place photo, Trip Advice sends the search or place request to Google Maps Platform through the TREK host. Google may process device and request information under the Google Privacy Policy. Trip Advice does not expose its Google credential to your browser.

Sharing

The trip owner can see contributions submitted through their link. We share information with service providers only when needed to operate, secure, or maintain Trip Advice, or when law requires it.

Retention and deletion

Guest-requested erasure is immediate. After a share link expires or is disabled, Trip Advice keeps its feedback for up to 90 days, then deletes it automatically. Security and operational logs may remain for a limited period when needed to protect the service or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may use the erase control on the shared page to delete feedback associated with your browser session. You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. We may need enough information to verify the request.

Security and international use

We use access controls and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No system is completely secure. If you access Trip Advice outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to al@unsold.group.
