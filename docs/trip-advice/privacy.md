# Trek Trip Advice Privacy Policy

Draft for operator approval. Not yet effective. Prepared 13 September 2026.

## Scope and responsibility

This notice covers Trip Advice at https://trips.unsold.group, not all Trek features or other installations.
Before this notice takes effect, the operator must confirm its legal identity, contact details, and applicable data-protection obligations.

## Information processed

Trip Advice processes the information a trip owner selects for sharing, such as a public title, cities, and selected places or schedule items.
It also processes guest display names, suggestions, comments, selected place identifiers, and moderation decisions.
Authenticated owner actions are associated with the owner's Trek account.

The service uses a guest session identifier, a session cookie, and request-security tokens to authorize guest actions and prevent abuse.
Guest sessions expire after at most 30 days, or sooner when the shared link expires.
The application uses a hash of the requesting IP address for rate limiting.
Hosting and network services may separately process IP addresses and request metadata.
The operator must confirm the actual logging and retention settings before publication.

## Purposes and access

Information is used to display shared trips, receive and review advice, maintain sessions, and protect the service.
Anyone with a shared link may see the information the owner publishes. A link is not a guarantee of confidentiality.
Trip owners and authorized administrators can access feedback needed to manage the trip and service.
An accepted suggestion may become a place in the owner's private trip.

Do not include sensitive personal information in contributions or forward private trip links without permission.

## Google Places and external services

When enabled, Google Places receives search text, a search-session token, geographic search preferences, and identifiers for requested places or photos.
Google uses that information to supply search results and place content.
The [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), incorporated here by reference, explains Google's processing.
Following a Google Maps or other external link takes you to a service with its own terms and privacy practices.

These policy documents are hosted on GitHub. Visiting them is subject to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The operator must confirm hosting providers, processing locations, and any international-transfer arrangements before this notice becomes effective.

## Retention and deletion

Session expiry does not by itself guarantee that all feedback, logs, backups, or accepted trip places have been erased.
Trip Advice includes guest-feedback erasure and owner purge operations. Accepted places are separate trip records.
Before publication, the operator must set and document retention periods for feedback, logs, backups, and accepted records, including deletion exceptions.

## Your choices and rights

You can stop using a shared link and clear its browser cookies; clearing cookies does not delete server-side contributions.
Use the service's deletion controls where available, or contact the operator privately for help.
Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability.
You may also have a right to complain to a data-protection authority.
The operator must provide a private request channel and identify applicable legal bases before publication.

## Updates

The effective date will identify the approved version. Material changes must be communicated where required by law.
Do not send personal information or private trip links through public GitHub issues.
